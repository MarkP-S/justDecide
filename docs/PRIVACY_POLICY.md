# Privacy Policy — JustDecide

**Effective date:** 18 May 2026  
**Last updated:** 18 May 2026

Pearsonality Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the **JustDecide** mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

If you have questions, contact us at **support@pearsonality.com**.

---

## 1. Summary

- JustDecide **does not require an account**.
- Your wheel presets (names, options, weights, and theme settings) are stored **on your device only**.
- We **do not sell** your personal information.
- We **do not** use third-party advertising or analytics in the App as currently released.

---

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Information you provide in the App

When you use JustDecide, you may create and store:

- Wheel preset names  
- Option labels (text you enter)  
- Option weights (for slice size and spin odds)  
- Theme colour preferences  
- Spin results shown on screen (derived from your options; not sent to our servers)

This content is **user-generated** and is processed **locally on your device** to provide App functionality (create, edit, save, load, and spin wheels).

### 2.2 Information we do not collect

We do **not** ask for or require:

- Name, email address, or phone number to use the App  
- Location data  
- Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access for core features  
- Payment or billing information (the App is not described here as a paid product; update this section if you add purchases)

We do **not** operate user accounts for JustDecide and do **not** receive your wheel data on our servers.

### 2.3 Device storage

Wheel data is saved using **local storage on your device** (for example, via the platform’s on-device storage APIs used by the App). Data remains on your device until you change or delete it in the App, or until you uninstall the App.

**Uninstalling the App** typically removes locally stored App data from your device.

---

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

- Display and manage your wheels and options  
- Run spin animations and show results  
- Remember your saved wheels and settings between sessions  

We do **not** use your wheel content for advertising profiling.

---

## 4. Sharing of information

We **do not sell** your personal information.

We **do not share** your wheel presets or option text with other users or with Pearsonality Ltd’s servers, because that data is stored on your device.

We may disclose information only if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect our rights, users, or the public, where permitted by law.

---

## 5. Third parties

### 5.1 App distribution

If you download JustDecide from **Google Play** or another app store, that store may collect information according to its own privacy policy (for example, relating to your account, device, or downloads). We do not control the store’s data practices.

### 5.2 Open-source and platform software

The App is built using standard mobile frameworks and libraries (including Expo and React Native). Those components run on your device as part of the App. They do not receive your wheel content from us, because we do not host that content.

### 5.3 Links opened from the App

The App may open **external links** in your browser (for example, when you open this Privacy Policy from the About screen). Those websites are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to read them when you leave the App.

### 5.4 Analytics, crash reporting, and advertising

**As of the effective date above**, we do not integrate third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs in JustDecide. If we add any in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and describe what is collected and by whom.

---

## 6. Network use

Core wheel features work **without** creating an account and **without** sending your wheel presets to Pearsonality Ltd.

The App may use the network when you:

- Open external links (such as this Privacy Policy) in a browser  
- Install or update the App through an app store  

If a future version loads optional content from the internet (for example, sound effects from a remote URL), we will update this policy to describe that use. **The current version is intended to keep your wheel data on-device.**

---

## 7. Permissions

JustDecide requests only permissions needed for its features. We do not use permissions to access unrelated personal data (such as your contacts or precise location) for the features described in this policy.

If platform permissions change in a future release, we will update this policy and the store listing accordingly.

---

## 8. Children’s privacy

JustDecide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact **support@pearsonality.com** and we will take appropriate steps.

---

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to design the App so your wheel data stays on your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device (for example, screen lock and not sharing your device with others if your wheels are sensitive).

---

## 10. Your choices and rights

Because your wheel data is stored **on your device**, you can:

- **Edit or delete** presets and options in the App  
- **Remove all local App data** by uninstalling the App  

Depending on where you live (for example, the UK, EEA, or California), you may have additional rights regarding personal data. Because we do not maintain user accounts or receive your wheel content on our servers, many requests can be fulfilled directly on your device as described above. To exercise rights or ask questions, email **support@pearsonality.com**.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights where applicable law requires that protection.

---

## 11. International users

Pearsonality Ltd is based in the United Kingdom. If you use the App from other countries, your information is processed on your device under your control. Any contact you initiate with us (such as support email) may be processed in the UK or where our service providers operate.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new **Effective date** / **Last updated** date. Continued use of the App after changes become effective means you accept the updated policy, unless applicable law requires otherwise.
